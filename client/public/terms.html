Reward amounts are set in U.S. dollars per harvest (including any beginner boost) and are converted into NVDA at the time of the harvest using a Chainlink price feed. Rewards are paid from a smart contract that we fund at our discretion. Withdrawals are subject to a minimum amount, a maximum per withdrawal and a daily budget shared by all players. We do not guarantee that the pool will be funded at any particular time or level.

To keep rewards fair, each farm can earn NVDA from a limited number of harvests per day (later harvests that day still pay Coins), and a new farm must wait a few days before its first withdrawal. The current numbers are shown on the Rewards screen.
